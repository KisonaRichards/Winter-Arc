Levly Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Levly ("the app", "we") is a habit-building game. This policy explains what information the app handles and what it does not.

The short version

Levly has no accounts and no servers that receive your data. What you enter stays on your own device. We do not sell your information, show ads, or track you.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following in your device's local storage (inside the app or browser):

  • Your profile: name, height, weight, goals and the habits you choose;
  • Your progress: levels, XP, coins, streaks, purchases, daily mood notes and weekly reports;
  • Proof photos you choose to take or select for a habit. Photos stay on your device and are not uploaded by the app.

We cannot see this information. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, it is deleted. You can also use Settings → Reset Levly & Start Fresh at any time to erase it. The Export Backup feature creates a file that is saved wherever you choose; keep it private, because it contains your data.

Camera and photos

The app asks to use your camera or photo library only when you add a photo as proof for a habit. You can decline and use a different proof method.

Third-party services

To display the app, it loads styles, icons and fonts from third-party content providers (Tailwind CSS, Font Awesome via cdnjs/Cloudflare, and Google Fonts). When these load, those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address and device type, as with any website. No habit, profile or photo data is sent to them.

Sharing

The Share button opens your device's share sheet with a link to the game. We do not receive any information about who you share it with.

Children

Levly is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If we add features that handle more data (for example accounts or friends), we will update this policy and the app before collecting anything new.
